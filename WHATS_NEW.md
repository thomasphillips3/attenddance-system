# What's New in AttenDANCE

## Email more than one class (October 2026)

- **Pick several classes.** On Messages, choose Parents in Classes and tick as many as you want.
- **By day.** Tap Monday to tick every Monday class. Tap Tuesday too for both days.
- **By location.** Tap Oak Park or Lathrup Village to tick every class held there.
- **One email per family.** A parent with dancers in two picked classes still gets one copy. The form shows how many addresses it will reach before you send.

---

## PayPal checkout links (October 2026)

- **Use your PayPal checkout link.** Paste a PayPal checkout link (https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/...) into Payment Methods, for the studio or the Company. Parents get a "Pay with PayPal" button and are told the amount to enter. PayPal.me links still work too.

---

## Billing fixes (October 2026)

- **One payment, several charges.** Check every charge a payment covers. Change an amount to pay part of a charge, like $100 toward a $200 costume.
- **See what's cleared.** On the ledger, each charge shows Paid with a check, or what's left. Linked payments show a check and list the charges they paid.
- **Edit instead of delete.** Click the pencil on a payment or charge to fix the amount, date, method, or linked charges. Every edit is in the Audit Log.
- **Other** is now a Company charge category.
- **PayPal.** Turn on PayPal in Payment Methods for the studio, the Company, or both. Parents get a PayPal.me link with the amount filled in.

---

## Tap-In: tag check-in (October 2026)

Dancers can tap a key fob or card on a staff phone to check in and out. No more calling roll at the door.

- **Tap in, tap out.** First tap checks them in to their class, a second tap later checks them out. Back-to-back classes just work.
- **Any staff phone.** iPhones read the tag and tap a banner. An Android phone or tablet runs a hands-free station that keeps working if the Wi-Fi drops.
- **Parents get a heads up.** An email when their dancer checks in and out. They can turn it off, or sign up for texts, in their portal.
- **Easy fixes.** Undo is on every screen. A lost tag gets replaced in one click and stops working right away.
- **Who needs a tag**, **who never tapped out**, and a quiet "Forms needed" badge for staff.

To get started we order a few sample tags, test them, then do a short pilot with a class or two before handing tags out to everyone. Details are in the Tap-In section of the manual.

## What you can do now

**Money stuff.** Parents pay right from their portal — Zelle, Cash App, or a Square card invoice — and they see exactly what they owe. When someone pays you by Zelle or Cash App, they tap "I sent a payment" and it shows up in your **Pending Payments** inbox. One click to confirm and their balance updates. No more tracking who paid in your head. You've also got family payment splitting, payment plans for folks who need to spread it out, late fees when you want them (never automatic), monthly balance reminders, year-end statements, and donation receipts for the Foundation.

**Recital & costumes.** Track costumes per class, store each dancer's size, charge the costume fee to everyone at once, and see who's paid. Sell and count recital tickets too.

**The Company.** Run LSODance Company rosters, online auditions families sign up for, and performance schedules with call times — all of it shows up in the parent portal.

**Forms & families.** Parents sign waivers, photo release, and medical forms online, and you can see who still hasn't. Mark skills and print certificates. Parents request makeups, you schedule 'em.

**Growing the studio.** Share a sign-up link and families register themselves — you approve with one click. Waitlists for full classes. A leads list to track new families from "just asking" to enrolled. A weekly calendar. And an Insights page that shows who's active, who's slipping, and your enrollment trends.

Works on your phone too — add it to your home screen and it acts like an app.
