1. Start here

What this system does

AttenDANCE is the studio's own system for enrollment, attendance, tuition, parent communication, recital planning, and company management. Everything lives at attenddance.fly.dev and works on a phone, tablet, or laptop - no app to install.

Who can do what

There are three kinds of accounts, and the menu changes depending on which one you're signed in as.

Role Can do Cannot do
Admin Everything: money, students, staff, settings, recital, company -
Teacher Take attendance, manage check-in tags and photos, view students, families, classes, calendar and rules, send class or individual parent messages, edit recital numbers and cast, clock in and out, read this manual Anything to do with money, adding or editing students, settings, staff
Parent See only their own dancers: balance, attendance, pay, sign forms, initial rules, request makeups, sign up for auditions See anyone else's child, or any staff page
{% call tip("Give people the smallest role that works") %} A teacher account cannot see balances or edit the roster, so handing one to a new instructor is safe. Only give someone Admin if they need to touch money or settings. {% endcall %}

Signing in and your password

  1. Go to attenddance.fly.dev and sign in with your username and password.
  2. To change your own password: click your name (top right) then Your profile.
  3. Forgot it? Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page. It emails you a link that works for one hour.

Password recovery needs email delivery to be configured. If the link does not arrive, check spam and ask an admin for help. Use Sign out when leaving a shared device.

Teacher daily checklist

  1. Open Dashboard for today's classes and recent check-ins. Calendar shows the weekly schedule.
  2. Open the dancer's page from Students to review emergency contacts and health notes before class.
  3. Clock In on Time Clock when you begin work.
  4. Use Take Attendance or Tap-In. Check the result before assuming a mark saved.
  5. Review Today's taps for missing check-outs; use Messages for class updates.
  6. Clock Out when finished. Ask an admin to handle roster changes, payments, makeups, and skills.

Admin setup checklist

  1. Add staff accounts and locations, then create the season, its dates, and classes.
  2. Configure Payment Methods for Studio and Company separately; test email and any Square or SMS connection before relying on them.
  3. Create waivers and rules, open enrollment for the correct season, and approve registrations.
  4. Set up tuition rules, parent logins, and check-in tags. Review Pending for payments and activity.
  5. Before a season change or bulk billing, download a backup under Payment Methods → Data & Backup.

Finding things on a phone or computer

On a phone, open the menu button to see the sidebar. On wider screens, the top search finds students, families, and classes; type at least two characters and select a result. The Dashboard's No tag yet tile opens Tap-In. The User Manual link is in the sidebar and on the Dashboard for both staff roles.

You can add the site to your phone's Home Screen from your browser menu. Most features need an internet connection. Only the tap station has a queue for temporary outages; a saved Home Screen icon does not make the rest of the portal work offline.

2. Seasons

Admin only: creating, editing, activating, and deleting seasons. Teachers can browse the class schedule.

A season is a session of the year: Fall 2026, Summer 2027, and so on. This is what lets you build next season's whole schedule while the current one is still running, while keeping automatic tuition billing off until the season is active.

A season is always in one of three states:

  • Active - the season running right now. Exactly one season is active at a time. This is what parents, teachers, and check-in see.
  • Upcoming (draft) - a staging area for next season. Its classes stay off the live schedule, but you can publish them for early registration. Auto-billing is paused; admins can still post manual charges.
  • Archived - a finished season, kept as history.

Building next season while this one runs

  1. Go to Classes. At the top there's a season bar with a dropdown.
  2. Click New Season, name it (for example "Fall 2026"), and set its start and end dates - those dates decide how many weeks the attendance card shows, so set them. The page switches you into the new season automatically.
  3. While that season is selected in the dropdown, click Add Class for each class. Every class you add lands in the season you're viewing.
  4. Set up its tuition too if you want (see Auto-billing). Draft classes appear in billing dropdowns marked "(upcoming)"; their recurring rules do not post charges until activation.
  5. Switch the dropdown back to the active season any time to get back to what's live today.

Changing a season's dates or name

Pick the season in the dropdown and click Edit Dates. Works on any season, including the one that's running. The season bar shows the dates under the dropdown, or "No dates set" when there aren't any.

{% call danger("Don't make a new season just to add dates") %} If the season you're running has no dates, use Edit Dates on it. Creating a "Fall 2026" and activating it looks like the same thing but is not: activating cancels every class in the current season and stops their auto-billing. {% endcall %}

Turning the new season on

When the new session actually starts, select it in the dropdown and click Make This the Active Season.

{% call warn("Activating winds down the old season automatically") %} The season you're leaving gets archived, and all of its classes are cancelled the same way the Cancel Class button does it: auto-billing for those classes stops, enrollments close, and waitlists clear. Past attendance and everyone's balances are kept. If a class genuinely runs across both seasons, add it to the new season too - it will not carry itself over. {% endcall %}

Opening registration for next season early

You can take fall sign-ups while summer is still running. Go to Enrollment. Next to the "Registration open" switch there's a for dropdown - pick the upcoming season. The public sign-up link will then show only that season's classes.

3. Classes & schedule

Adding and editing a class

Admin only: class changes, enrollment changes, waitlists, and locations. Teachers can view classes and the calendar.

Classes in the menu, then Add Class. You set the name, day, start and end time, level, location, instructor, and maximum number of students. The maximum is enforced - once a class is full, new sign-ups go to a waitlist instead.

Use Edit on a class card to change any of that later, including mid-season. Manage Students on the card is where you add or remove dancers.

Waitlists

Open Manage Students → Waitlist on the class. Add to Waitlist queues an existing dancer without enrolling them. When a place opens, use Enroll on the waitlist entry; the class must have space. Remove an entry if the family no longer wants the place. Removing a dancer from a roster does not automatically promote someone from the waitlist.

Cancelling a class

{% call warn("Cancel Class does more than hide it") %} It stops that class's auto-billing so families don't keep getting charged, removes it from dancers' class lists, and clears its waitlist. Past attendance and past charges stay on the books. Use this when a class stops running - it is the right button, but it is not a quiet one. {% endcall %}

Calendar

Calendar shows the whole week laid out by day and time. It's the fastest way to spot a double-booking or an empty evening.

Locations

Locations is where you set up the community centers and rooms you teach in - name, address, phone, and notes (parking, door codes, hours). Once a location exists you can pick it on any class.

{% call danger("Deactivating a location cannot be undone from the app") %} There's no "show inactive" list and no reactivate button, so a deactivated location is gone from the screen for good. The app also won't stop you deactivating one that still has classes in it - check the "N active classes" count on the card first. {% endcall %}

4. Students & families

Adding a dancer

Students then Add Student (admins only). The form is long on purpose - it holds everything you'd otherwise keep on paper:

  • Basic info - name, birthday, school, grade
  • Parent and emergency contact - the parent email here is the address used for receipts, reminders, and message blasts, so it's worth getting right
  • Family - links siblings together (see below)
  • Sizes & measurements - 16 fields for costume ordering, plus a notes box. Fill these in once and they show up automatically when you're assigning costumes.
  • Health - allergies, special needs, medical notes. Anything you put here shows in a red callout on the dancer's page so a teacher can't miss it.

The dancer's page

Click a dancer to open their detail page: contact and emergency info, enrolled classes, health flags, recent attendance, and (for admins) their account balance with a link to the full ledger, plus the Portal Access card for parent logins.

Withdrawing a dancer

The trash icon deactivates rather than deletes - their history, attendance, and ledger all stay. You can reactivate them later from their detail page. There is no way to permanently delete a student from the app, and that's deliberate.

Families

A family groups siblings. On the Families page you see each family, its dancers, and (admins only) the combined balance with a link to a family ledger - every sibling's charges and payments, with a separate running balance for each fund. Use Add Family to create a household, then attach a dancer by editing the dancer and picking the family.

The one thing families change for parents: if a parent has two or more dancers in the same family and the family owes money, the portal offers them a single "pay for the whole family" option, and the payment gets split across the kids automatically.

{% call warn("Family balances only count active dancers") %} If you deactivate a dancer who still owes money, that debt disappears from the family balance - but it stays on the Aging (A/R) report tagged "Withdrawn." If a family total looks too low, check the Aging report. {% endcall %}

Export the roster

Export CSV on the Students page downloads the roster (names, family, birthday, parent contact, emergency contact, allergies, special needs). Admins also get a balance column. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets - handy for a mail merge or a printed emergency contact sheet for the front desk.

5. Enrollment & the parent portal

Public sign-up (new families)

Admin only: enrollment approval, parent login invitations, and reset links.

  1. Go to Enrollment and turn on Registration open. Pick which season it's for if you're taking sign-ups for an upcoming one.
  2. Share the public link (it's at the top of that page - attenddance.fly.dev/register). Put it on the website, Instagram, wherever.
  3. Families fill it out themselves. New requests show a badge on the Enrollment menu item.
  4. Open each request and Approve it. That creates the family, creates the dancers, and enrolls them in the classes they picked.

Review requested classes and contact details before approval. A full class puts the dancer on its waitlist. Use the queue's status filter to revisit approved or rejected requests; Reject leaves the request on record without creating a login or enrolling anyone.

Returning families use the exact same link. The system matches them by email, so it re-enrolls existing dancers instead of creating duplicates, and flags the request Returning in your queue.

The login page also points new families at the same form ("New Parent? Click here to register"), so a parent who lands there by mistake isn't stuck.

What the form asks for

Required, and the form won't submit without them: parent name, email, and phone; an emergency contact name and phone (the relationship is optional); and for each dancer, first and last name, date of birth, and allergies / medical needs / other notes. That last one is required on purpose - a blank used to be ambiguous between "no allergies" and "didn't answer", so parents are asked to type "None" when there's nothing. Answers like "None", "n/a", or "no known allergies" are filed as no allergy, so the red allergy warning on a dancer's page keeps meaning something.

Optional: a second parent/guardian, home address, the emergency contact's relationship to the dancer, and the dancer's own email.

Approving files all of it automatically. The address and second guardian go on the family; the emergency contact, dancer email, and allergies go on each dancer.

{% call warn("Re-registration refreshes your records") %} When a family registers again, a new address or second guardian replaces what's on file - that's how a move gets picked up. Blanks never erase anything. A returning dancer's emergency contact and allergies are never overwritten, so anything you corrected by hand stands. If the form disagrees with what's on file about allergies, the approve message says CHECK: and shows you both answers - update the dancer by hand after you've decided which is right. {% endcall %}

Two dancers can't share the same dancer email. If a family puts one address on both siblings, the first gets it, and the approve message tells you which ones were left blank.

Parent portal accounts

Approving a registration now sets the family's login up for you. It creates one account for the household - covering every dancer on it, not one per child - and emails the parent a link to pick their own password. You don't have to do anything.

  • A family that already has a login doesn't get a second one; a newly approved sibling just appears on the login they already use.
  • The approve message tells you whether the email went out. If email isn't configured, it hands you the link to send yourself instead.
  • Nothing is sent until you approve, so a request you reject never creates an account.

Families from before this was automatic

Anyone approved earlier still needs one. On Enrollment, a Parent logins card appears whenever families are missing an account. It tells you how many, and Send logins emails all of them the sign-up link at once.

  • Safe to run more than once. Families who already log in are skipped, and anyone who hasn't used their link yet gets the same one again - so it doubles as a nudge.
  • Families with no email on file are skipped and named, so you can add an address and re-run.
  • The card disappears once every family has a login.

Inviting by hand (a dancer you typed in yourself)

A dancer you added directly, rather than through the sign-up form, has no registration to approve - so give that parent an invite code:

  1. Open the dancer's page and find the Portal Access card.
  2. Click Generate invite code. You get a link on screen.
  3. Copy it and text or email it to the parent yourself. The app does not send it.
  4. The parent opens the link, enters their name, email, and a password, and they're in - already connected to their dancer.

Each code works once and then stops working. Siblings each get their own code, and when a parent redeems a second code with the same email, the system adds that dancer to their existing login instead of making a second account.

{% call warn("Send the link, not just the code") %} The link you copy has the code built into it, so the parent lands on the sign-up form with it already filled in. The login page no longer mentions invite codes at all - it only points new families at the public sign-up form - so a parent who has only the bare code and not the link has no obvious way in. If someone loses theirs, generate a fresh one and send the link again. (This is only for dancers you entered by hand. Approving a registration emails the link on its own.) {% endcall %}

A parent who's locked out

Same Portal Access card, click Reset password link next to their name, and text them the link. It expires in one hour. Parents can also use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page themselves now that email is set up.

{% call tip("Manually generated links must be sent by staff") %} Manual invite links and staff-generated reset links appear on your screen only. Copy them before you navigate away - if you lose one, just generate a fresh one. {% endcall %}

What parents see

Their dancers, each one's balance and payment history, attendance, class schedule, recital program, and buttons to pay, sign forms, initial the rules, request a makeup, view a progress certificate, and sign up for auditions. They cannot see any other family.

6. Attendance & makeups

Taking attendance

  1. Open Take Attendance on a tablet or laptop. Teachers can do this - they don't need an admin account.
  2. Pick the class. Today's classes are listed first.
  3. Tap each dancer's purple box - that's this week - to mark them. Tap once for P (present), twice for A (absent), three times for T (tardy), four times for E (excused absence), five times to clear it back to blank. It saves as you go, so there's no Save button to forget.

The card shows one box per week of the season, labelled with the date that class meets, and opens scrolled to this week. Drag sideways to see earlier weeks. Mark Everyone Present only affects this week.

Missed taking attendance? Tap the dashed box under the week you missed. It records the dancer as P, A, T or E (same taps) on the day that class met (for a Thursday class, that Thursday) and logs who filled it in. Tap through to blank to undo. Greyed-out boxes are weeks still to come, or weeks before the season started, and can't be tapped.

{% call tip("The card only shows the whole season once the season has dates") %} With no start and end dates on the season, the card shows just the last 8 weeks and past weeks can't be tapped. Set the dates under Classes › Edit Dates (see Seasons). {% endcall %}

Attendance Log shows the history and lets you check a dancer in manually if you missed them.

{% call warn("Take attendance even on light nights") %} The Insights page flags any dancer with no attendance record in the last 30 days as "at risk." If a class simply never gets attendance taken, every dancer in it shows up as at risk and the number is meaningless. This is the most common false alarm in the system. {% endcall %}

Tap-In: tag check-in

Dancers can check themselves in and out by tapping a key fob or card on a staff phone. A tap marks them present on the card (a teacher's P, A, T or E still works the same), and a second tap at least 5 minutes later checks them out with the default settings. Everything lives under Tap-In in the menu.

  1. Give a dancer a tag: open their page, then Check-in Tags › Set up a tag. On an Android phone, hold a blank tag to the phone and follow the steps. On an iPhone, copy the link into the free NXP TagWriter app, write it, then tap the tag and choose Finish setup.
  2. Drop-off: on an Android phone or tablet, open Tap-In › Start a tap station and tap Start. Dancers tap one after another. On an iPhone, hold the top of the phone to the tag and tap the banner.
  3. Lost tag: on the dancer's page, choose Replace. The old tag stops working right away.
  4. Who still needs a tag: the Tap-In page lists them. Today's taps shows who's here and who never tapped out.
{% call tip("Mistakes are one tap to fix") %} Every result screen has Undo, and a dancer who checked out by accident can be put back in class. A tap with no class at that time asks you to pick the class. {% endcall %} {% call warn("Never write a dancer's name on a tag") %} Write the tag's label (like T-0014) on its box instead. If a tag is lost, whoever finds it learns nothing about the dancer. {% endcall %}

Parents get an email when their dancer checks in and out, and can turn it off or sign up for texts in their portal. Admins control all of this in Tap-In › Settings, including a pause switch.

Tapping a card or fob: what to do and what to expect

The cards and fobs are NFC tags, a short-range kind of RFID. A tag has no battery and no screen. Most tags hold one private link, and they work when held right up against the back of a staff phone or tablet. A tag set up as USB-only is saved by its serial number and gets no link, so it will not open on an iPhone. It works on the USB reader and on the Android tap station, which reads its serial number. Only use a blank tag for this: setup does not erase what is already on a tag, so a tag that was written before can still open its old link on a phone, and the Android station will follow that old link instead of the serial number and may reject the tag. A tag does not track anyone, and it does not work from across the room.

How to tap

  1. Hold the tag flat against the back of the phone, touching or within about an inch.
  2. Hold it still for a second or two, then wait for the screen. Tapping again and again does not make it faster, and a repeat read within a few seconds (10 by default, set in Tap-In settings) is ignored anyway.
  3. One tag at a time. Two tags together, or a tag stacked with a bank or transit card, can confuse the reader.
  4. Wait for the result before the next dancer steps up, so staff can see whose tap it was.

Where on the phone

  • iPhone (XS or newer): the reader is at the top edge of the back. A banner appears after the tag is read; tap it to open the result. If nothing appears, close the Camera and Wallet apps, wake the phone, and try again.
  • Android phone or tablet: the reader is usually near the middle or upper part of the back, but it varies by model. Find the spot once by sliding a tag slowly across the back until the station reacts, then tell everyone where it is. No banner on the station: the result just appears.
  • iPad: cannot read tags by itself. Use an iPhone, an Android device, or the USB reader.
  • USB reader: touch the tag to the reader's marked surface. The result appears on the laptop or tablet it is plugged into. A USB-only tag also works on the Android station, but not on an iPhone.
{% call tip("If a tap won't read, check these first") %} A thick or metal phone case, a magnetic wallet or ring stuck to the back, a tag held against a metal surface, a screen that is off or locked, and a tag that isn't touching the phone are the usual causes. Take the case off or move the tag a little. On an Android station, check the page is still open and shows NFC on. {% endcall %}

What the result screen means

Screen It means What to do
Green: IN Checked in to the class shown. If it says Already checked in at ..., they tapped again soon after checking in (within 5 minutes by default) and nothing changed. Nothing. Check the name and class are right.
Blue: OUT Checked out of the class shown. Nothing. If it was a mistake, choose Put back in class.
Amber: PICK No class for this dancer right now (too early, too late, or not their day). Choose the right class from the list. Nothing is recorded until you do.
Amber: OUT, Already checked out They already left this class. Choose Put back in class if they came back, or pick another class.
Amber: ! The tag belongs to a dancer who is marked inactive. Ask an admin to reactivate the dancer, or mark them on the attendance card.
Red: NO The tag is not recognized, was reported lost, is retired, was never finished, or is not assigned to a dancer. Mark them on the attendance card, then look the tag up on the dancer's page.
Red: NOT RECORDED The phone could not confirm the tap (no connection, or an error). It may or may not have saved: the tap can reach the server and the reply get lost. Open Today's taps, find the dancer, and compare the In and Out times with the tap you just made. A dancer being listed is not enough: a check-out that failed still shows them as "still here". If the time you expect is there, leave it. If not, tap again, or mark the attendance card.

A name followed by a yellow Forms needed badge means paperwork is missing; it never stops a check-in. On the Android station you also hear a short sound: two rising notes for IN, two falling notes for OUT, a low buzz for a problem, and one soft beep when a tap was saved for later. The screen clears by itself and goes back to Tap your tag.

Younger dancers

  • For the littlest dancers, a teacher can take the fob from their bag and tap it for them. A fob clipped to a zipper or backpack strap is easier to keep track of than a card.
  • Teens and adults can tap their own card on the station.
  • The tap records attendance. It does not confirm who is picking a child up, so keep following the studio's normal pickup rules.

What to tell families

  1. Bring the tag to every class, on the dance bag or zipper. Keep it out of the wash.
  2. Tap in when you arrive and tap out when you leave.
  3. Tap once and wait for the green or blue screen.
  4. Don't bend, drill, or stick things on the tag. Don't write on a fob.
  5. If a tag is lost or damaged, tell the front desk right away. It is switched off immediately and a new one is set up, and the old one can't be used by anyone who finds it.
  6. Parents can turn the check-in email on or off in the parent portal, and sign up for texts too once the studio has turned texts on.

Tap-In setup, tag status, and photos

Use blank NTAG213 (or NTAG215) fobs or cards. Test a few from every new batch with the free NXP TagInfo app before handing any out, since some cheap listings carry fake chips. Keep pilot tags unlocked. Android Chrome writes the link and asks for a second tap to verify it; with an external writer, the link is shown once, so write it before closing setup. An unfinished tag stays Pending and does not check anyone in. Finish setup from the written tag, or delete the pending entry and start again.

Under Check-in Tags, Lost and Retire disable a tag immediately. Replace marks the old tag lost and starts a new one. Found it, reactivate enables a lost or retired tag again. A dancer can have more than one tag. Add photo, Change, and Remove manage a staff-only check-in photo so the tap screen can help identify the dancer.

{% call warn("Locking a tag is permanent") %} An admin must first save the Permanent tag address in Tap-In Settings. Lock makes the physical tag read-only; it cannot be rewritten even if you change the website address later. Keep the old address working if the site moves. {% endcall %}

Tap stations and connection problems

  • iPhone: sign in as staff in Safari, even if already signed in through a Home Screen icon. Keep the phone awake; close Camera and Wallet. Hold its top edge to the tag and open the banner.
  • Android: enable NFC, use Chrome, open the station, press Start, and keep it open.
  • USB reader: choose Scan on the USB reader during setup if enrolling a serial-only tag. That tag gets no link, so it works on the USB station and the Android station but not on an iPhone. Use a blank tag, because an old link already on the tag can make the Android station reject it. Configure the reader for the full NTAG213 serial: 14 hexadecimal characters, normal byte order, no separators, and Enter at the end.

The station can queue taps during a temporary outage. Keep that browser page open and reconnect so the queue can upload. The N saved, not sent counter disappears when the queue empties. If an upload error says a saved tap could not be recorded, check and correct the attendance card; an empty queue alone does not prove every tap succeeded. NOT RECORDED means the phone could not confirm the tap, so it may or may not have saved. A regular iPhone tag page cannot queue a tap offline: check the dancer's In and Out times on Today's taps, then retry online or use the attendance card. Do not treat an offline message as proof the dancer checked in, or as proof they didn't.

Reading and correcting tap results

By default, check-in opens 30 minutes before class and closes at its end. Repeat reads within 10 seconds are ignored, and a second tap within 5 minutes of check-in says the dancer is already in. Both delays are settings an admin can change in Tap-In settings, so if taps behave differently, check the numbers there. With back-to-back carry-over enabled, a later check-out also records the intervening consecutive classes. If a tap asks for a class, select the right one before leaving the result screen.

Use Undo for a wrong tap, or Put back in class for an accidental check-out. Today's taps has date navigation, class sessions, missing check-outs after class ends, and refused taps that need attention. An unknown, lost, retired, or unfinished tag needs staff attention; a non-staff phone always sees a generic tag page without dancer information.

The staff-only Forms needed badge flags missing paperwork. Admins may also see Check account; teachers do not see that badge or dollar amounts. Neither badge prevents check-in.

Tap-In settings and household notices

Admin only: Tap-In Settings controls early/late windows, repeat-tap delay, minimum time before check-out, optional tardy marking (0 turns it off), and back-to-back carry-over. Save changes with Save settings.

Email notices are normally on. Parents can change household preferences in their portal; staff can toggle household email or turn texts off on a dancer's page. Staff cannot opt a parent into texts. To opt in, the parent enters a mobile number and verifies the texted code. The admin must first enable text notices with the studio's SMS service configured.

Notices are grouped briefly after taps, so siblings may share an email. Adult dancers, undone taps, and stale events more than 20 minutes old do not generate notices. An admin can pause all notices or set a daily message limit. A successful check-in does not guarantee an email or text was delivered.

Makeups

Admin only: scheduling and managing makeup requests and skills.

Parents can request a makeup from their portal; those land on the Makeups page marked Requested, and you use Schedule to set the class and date. If you're logging a makeup yourself with Log Makeup, it goes straight in as Scheduled. Mark it Complete when the dancer attends.

{% call danger("Delete on a makeup is permanent") %} Unlike most things here, this one really removes the record. The page also shows only the 200 most recent makeups, so old ones drop off the list over time. {% endcall %}

Skills and progress certificates

Skills lets you define what dancers can earn (with an optional category like "Ballet") and tick them off per dancer. Each dancer gets a printable Certificate of Achievement listing what they've earned - parents can see it from their portal as Progress. Nice for end of season.

Ticking a box saves instantly. Un-ticking one deletes that achievement with no confirmation, so be a little careful with the checkboxes.

7. Money

Admin only: all staff billing screens and settings in this section.

How billing works here

Every charge and every payment is recorded against one dancer. A balance is just charges minus payments. Family totals are a roll-up of the siblings' individual balances. All of this lives on the Payments page.

Studio and Company are separate

Every dancer has two accounts. Studio covers tuition, costumes, shoes, registration, and other studio fees. Company covers competition, convention, transportation, lodging, observer fees, costume rental, company dues, company tickets, and other Company costs. A charge's category selects its fund. When recording a payment, choose which balance it pays.

A Studio credit never pays a Company debt automatically. Student ledgers, family ledgers, statements, parent payment options, and reports keep the funds separate. The Company account appears for dancers with Company activity; it may be hidden for studio-only dancers.

Charging

  • Add Charge - one dancer, one amount, a category (tuition, costumes, shoes, registration fee, other) and a description.
  • Bulk Charge - charge everyone in a class at once. Use this for costume deposits, recital fees, that sort of thing.
  • Add Payment - choose the dancer, positive amount, date, category, method, and description, then save. Record only money actually received. For an explicit fund choice, use Record Payment on the dancer's ledger.

Apply a payment to specific charges

Use this when a payment is for particular items, such as a costume deposit and this month's tuition, rather than the oldest outstanding bill.

  1. Open Payments → Add Payment and choose the dancer. Or open that dancer's ledger and choose Record Payment.
  2. Under Apply to charges, check every charge the payment covers. Each row shows the date, description, what's left on it, and the fund. Only charges with money still owed appear.
  3. A checked charge fills in what's left on it. For a partial payment, change that charge's amount, for example $100 toward a $200 costume. If you typed the payment amount first, a newly checked charge takes no more than what's still unassigned.
  4. Check the payment amount, date, and method, add a note if helpful, then save. The line under the list says how much is going to which charges.

One payment can only pay charges in one fund. Once you check a Studio charge, Company charges grey out, and the other way round. If the checked amounts add up to less than the payment, the rest pays the oldest unpaid charges in that fund.

{% call tip("Nothing checked keeps automatic allocation") %} With nothing checked, the payment pays the oldest unpaid charges in the same fund. Choosing a category alone does not link a payment to an item. {% endcall %}

On the ledger, a charge row's Payment column shows Paid with a check when nothing is owed on it, or $X left when it is partly paid. A linked payment shows a check by its amount and a Linked: line naming each charge and how much went to it. The Billing list shows the same under each description.

{% call warn("A linked amount cannot be more than what's left on the charge") %} If another payment settled part of the item while the form was open, close and reopen the form to refresh what's left. Do not switch to another dancer's charge or record the payment twice to get past an error. {% endcall %}

Fix a payment or charge

Click the pencil on any row of the Billing list or a dancer's ledger to fix it in place: amount, date, category, method, note, and which charges a payment is linked to. Every edit is saved to the Audit Log with the old and new values.

  • The dancer and whether it's a charge or a payment can't be changed. Delete it and add it again.
  • A charge can't be lowered below what payments already cover on it, and can't move to the other fund while payments are linked to it. Unlink or lower those payments first.
  • Fund transfers have no pencil: they're two matched entries. Delete and move the money again.

Auto-billing (monthly tuition)

On the Payments page, Auto-Billing Rules is where monthly tuition gets set up. Add Rule, pick the class, the amount per dancer, the category, and the day of the month it should bill. From then on every enrolled dancer in that class is charged automatically on that day.

{% call tip("It will not surprise-charge anyone") %} Two protections are built in. A rule never bills for a month that already passed - the first charge is the first due date on or after you created the rule. And a rule on an upcoming season's class does not bill at all until you activate that season. That's what makes it safe to set fall tuition up in July. {% endcall %}

A dancer who joins mid-month is not automatically charged a partial month - post that one by hand if you want it.

Rules run when the app starts or its billing job runs, so review posted charges on the due day. A due day of 29–31 uses the last day in a shorter month. Editing a rule changes future runs; stopping a rule does not remove charges already posted.

How parents pay

Set the options up under Payment Methods:

  • Zelle - turn it on, set the display name, upload your QR code, and set the memo instruction telling parents to put their dancer's name in.
  • Cash App - turn it on and enter the cashtag. Parents get a link with the amount already filled in.
  • PayPal - turn it on and paste either a PayPal checkout link (paypal.com/ncp/payment/..., the kind PayPal makes for a "Pay" button) or your PayPal.me link or name. A PayPal.me link fills in the amount. A checkout link can't, so the portal tells parents what to type on PayPal's page.
  • Square (card) - paste your Square payment link and parents get a "Pay by card" button. That's all that's required. The Advanced section is only if you also want to email Square invoices from inside the app.

Zelle, Cash App, and PayPal payments don't tell the system anything on their own, so there's a confirmation step - see below.

Configure Company payment details separately on the same settings page: its own Zelle name and QR code, Cash App tag, PayPal link, and card payment link. The portal uses the chosen fund's destinations. Save with Save Settings.

Square invoices and connection settings

For emailed invoices, expand Square's Advanced settings, enter its access token, location ID, environment, and webhook signature key, then Test Connection. Sandbox is for testing. The token stays masked after saving; Clear token removes it. Ask Thomas to help connect email or SMS delivery when needed.

Open a dancer's ledger and use Send Invoice when Square is connected. This bills the outstanding Studio balance with a due date 14 days out. A paid invoice posts automatically when its verified Square notification arrives. A standalone card payment link does not itself reconcile a payment; verify and record it if needed. Company payments use the Foundation's link and are not included in Studio invoices.

The Pending inbox

When a parent pays by Zelle or Cash App they tap "I sent it" in the portal. That lands on the Pending page with a badge in the menu.

  1. Check your bank or Cash App that the money actually arrived.
  2. Confirm it - that records the payment on the dancer's ledger and emails the parent a receipt. For a family payment it splits across the siblings automatically.
  3. Reject it if you can't find the money. Nothing is posted.
{% call warn("Confirm means the money is really there") %} Confirming is what creates the payment record. Always verify against your actual bank or Cash App before you press it. {% endcall %}

Check the Fund column as well as the amount. Rejection can include a reason shown to the family. Use the status filter to revisit confirmed or rejected claims. Recent Activity below the inbox records who performed changes such as confirmation, charges, deletions, and transfers.

Late fees

Set your default amount under Payment Methods → Late Fees, then choose the amount and balance threshold on Pending → Apply Late Fees. Nothing charges a late fee automatically - you press the button when you want it. Any dancer who already got a late fee in the same fund this calendar month is skipped, so a double-click can't double-charge. Fees are assessed per dancer and per fund above the threshold.

Payment plans

On Pending → Payment Plans → New Plan, choose a dancer, installment amount, number of installments, due day (1–28), and optional note. This is a Studio payment schedule for that dancer. The first installment is this month's due day if it has not passed, otherwise next month's. Creating a new plan replaces that dancer's active plan.

Click an installment to toggle its paid marker; End plan closes the plan. These markers do not post payments, charge a card, or change the ledger. Record or confirm the actual payment separately. Parents see the schedule and the paid markers in their portal.

Correcting payments and moving credit

  1. Wrong amount, duplicate, or wrong dancer: use the trash icon on the posted transaction in Payments or the dancer's ledger, confirm deletion, and record the correct entry. Deletion is permanent and logged; it does not refund money through a bank or payment provider.
  2. Wrong fund on the same dancer: open the dancer's ledger → Move money between funds. Choose From, To, amount, and a note. It adds a charge in the source fund and payment in the destination with a shared reference. It does not move cash between bank accounts.
  3. One payment covered siblings: open Families, the family ledger, then Move credit between siblings. Choose the child holding credit, the receiving child, fund, amount, and note. Both must belong to that family, and the source must have enough credit. Two linked entries move it within the same fund, preserving the family total.

Transfers preserve the original payment. Check both ledger entries after saving. A suggested Move credit banner can fill in a sibling transfer for you.

To correct which charges a payment was applied to, click its pencil and change the checked charges. See Fix a payment or charge. Deleting a charge removes its payment links but keeps the payments; those payments then use the normal oldest-charge allocation. Review the remaining items and Aging after a correction.

Printable account statements

Parents open Statement on their dancer's portal card. Admins can open /students/STUDENT_ID/statement or /families/FAMILY_ID/statement, using the numeric ID from the dancer detail or family ledger address. Add ?year=2026 to choose a year, or use the year selector once the statement opens. For example, dancer ID 42's 2026 statement is /students/42/statement?year=2026. Statements show carry-forward, charges, payments, and closing balances separately for Studio and Company. Use the statement's Print button or the browser's print command to save a PDF.

8. Messages & reminders

Emailing parents

Messages sends email to one of three groups:

  • All Parents - admins only
  • Parents in a Class - everyone currently enrolled in it
  • Individual Student's Parent - one family

A family with three dancers gets one copy, not three. Message history is kept at the bottom of the page.

Seeing who it goes to, and proofing it first

  • As soon as you pick who to send to, the form says how many addresses that is. Show list shows every one; Download list saves them as a spreadsheet. With "All Parents" selected, that download is your everyone's-email report.
  • Send test to me emails the message - attachments and all - to your own address only, with [TEST] on the subject. Nothing is sent to parents and nothing goes in the history. Read it, fix anything, then hit Send.
  • Send a copy to me (checked by default) puts your own address on the real send, so you get exactly what the parents got, when they got it.
  • In Message History, click View on any message to see the full text and every address it went to, with Copy and Download buttons.
{% call warn("Check the status column after a blast") %} Sending happens in the background, so a message shows Saved first and flips to Sent when it goes out. If one is still saying "Saved" a while later, the send failed and there's no retry button - open it, copy the addresses, and send it from Gmail. {% endcall %}

This sends email only. There's no text-message blast.

Attaching a file

Use Choose Files under the message box to send a flyer, a permission slip, a costume order form, or a schedule along with the email. Every parent in the group gets a copy attached to their message.

  • Allowed: PDF, photos (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, HEIC), Word, Excel, PowerPoint, TXT, and CSV
  • Limits: up to 5 files, 5MB each, 10MB total per message
  • Click the × next to a file to take it off before sending

Attachments stay with the message in the history, so the file can be downloaded again later from the Attachments column - handy when a parent says they never got it. If email isn't configured yet, download it there and attach it by hand to the email you send from Gmail.

{% call warn("Big files bounce") %} Phone photos and scanned PDFs run large. If a flyer is over 5MB, shrink it first (export the PDF smaller, or resize the photo) - the studio's email provider rejects oversized attachments even when the system accepts them. {% endcall %}

Emailing both parents

Every email the system sends about a dancer - studio messages, the bill, payment receipts, balance reminders - goes to every parent address on file, not just one.

A dancer picks up addresses from two places:

  • On the dancer: Parent Email and Second Parent Email. Open the dancer from Students and edit them.
  • On the family: the main email and the second parent/guardian's. Families who signed up through the public form already have both, so most households are covered without you typing anything.

The same address listed twice only gets one copy, and capitalization doesn't matter. A dancer's own email is only used when there's no parent address at all, so bills never go to the child when a parent is on file.

Balance reminders

Two ways to nudge families who owe:

  • Right now: Send Reminders on the Pending page emails every family with a balance over $0.
  • Monthly: Payment Methods → Automatic Reminders, pick a day of the month and a minimum balance.
{% call warn("Automatic reminders need the app to be awake") %} The site sleeps when nobody's using it, and the monthly reminder only fires when the app is running on the day you picked. If nobody opens the site that day, that month's reminders quietly don't go out. Two easy habits fix this: pick a day you're normally working anyway, and open the site that morning. Ask Thomas to configure a scheduled reminder job if you need unattended delivery. {% endcall %}

Reminders go out one per family, based on what the whole family owes. If one child has a credit and a sibling has a matching balance, they cancel out and nobody gets a reminder. Studio and Company money are still counted separately.

One payment covered two kids but landed on one child's ledger? Open the family's ledger and tap Move credit between siblings (a yellow note offers it with the amount filled in). It adds a charge on the child who has the credit and a payment on the child who owes, sharing a reference. Nothing already recorded is changed.

Every reminder includes the link to the parent portal sign-in, so parents can tap straight through to pay.

Reminders can also go by text if you set up Twilio under Text Messages (SMS), but email alone is fine for most studios.

9. Forms, waivers & rules

Forms & Waivers

Admin only: creating and managing forms and rules. Teachers can view Rules and its acknowledgment status.

Create a form (liability waiver, photo release, medical authorization) with New Form: a title and the text the parent reads. Parents sign from their portal by typing their full name. Each form shows you a compliance card - how many have signed, and the names of who hasn't.

The checkbox "Allow parents to opt out while still signing" is for optional consents. Turn it on for a photo release so a parent can decline while still completing the form, and you get a recorded decision either way. Leave it off for anything mandatory like liability - the system will refuse a decline.

{% call warn("Pick the opt-out setting before you publish a form") %} It can't be changed after the form is created, because it would change what everyone's existing signatures meant. To switch it you have to archive the form and make a new one, and everyone signs again. {% endcall %}

Use a form's Edit control to correct its text or title; Archive removes it from the active forms list. Existing signatures are kept. If a policy changes enough to require a fresh signature, archive the old form and create a new one.

Rules

Rules is your studio policies, one per entry. Parents initial each rule individually from their portal, and you get a status table showing who's complete.

Use Add Rule to create a policy, and edit or remove existing entries as needed. Initials can't be undone by the parent once entered. Editing an existing rule keeps its acknowledgments; add a new rule when you need everyone to initial a new policy.

10. Recital & costumes

Recital Hub

Recital Hub is the command center for the June show. Create the year with New Year, then work through the tabs:

Choose the recital year from the picker. An admin uses Make Active to select the recital parents see; this is separate from the active class season.

  • Overview - title, theme, date, show times, venue, your director's note and acknowledgments (both print in the booklet).
  • Show Order - every number in performance order. Each one holds the class or company performing it, the music (song, artist, link, cue notes), the choreographer and formation notes, duration, props, and the cast. Reorder with the up and down arrows.
  • Awards - award title, category, and recipient.
  • Booklet & Ads - program ads and shout-outs, with sizes, prices, images, and a paid checkbox.

When a number is linked to a class or company, Fill from class/company adds the whole roster to the cast at once, skipping anyone already in it.

Open a number to edit its details, add or remove individual cast members, and record their parts and notes. Changes to a class roster do not replace your saved cast list.

Admin only: under Overview → Show dates & tickets, link a performance created in Company → Performances. This connects the show's ticket types and sales to the recital. Unlinking removes that connection.

Open Booklet produces the print-ready program: cover, director's note, the full program with cast lists, awards, sponsor ads, and acknowledgments. Print it or save it as a PDF to send to the printer.

{% call tip("Teachers can help build the show") %} Teachers can edit the show order, music, choreography notes, and cast - which is exactly the part they know best. Only admins can change the Overview, Awards, and Ads. {% endcall %} {% call danger("Deleting a recital takes everything with it") %} Numbers, cast lists, awards, and ads all go. There's no undo. {% endcall %}

Costumes & tickets

Admin only: costume fees, assignments, and ticket management.

From the Recital Hub, Costumes & Tickets has two tabs.

Costumes - create a costume with a fee and vendor, assign dancers (or add a whole class at once), and record each dancer's costume size. The sizes you entered on the dancer's record show right next to it for reference.

{% call warn("Charge fee posts real charges") %} The Charge fee button adds the costume fee to every assigned dancer's ledger. Dancers who were already charged get skipped, so it's safe to press again after adding more dancers - but you can't un-charge from this screen. To reverse one, go to the dancer's ledger. {% endcall %}

Tickets - set up ticket types with prices for each show date, then record sales as they come in with buyer, quantity, and paid status. Mark an order paid when the money arrives. Parents can order through their portal. The header keeps a running total of tickets sold and money collected versus pending. A ticket's paid marker is its sales record; it does not post a payment to a dancer's tuition ledger.

11. Company & auditions

Admin only: company rosters, auditions, and performances.

Company runs LSODance Company and any other performance group. Three tabs:

  • Companies - create a group and add dancers with a role (defaults to "Member").
  • Auditions - create an audition with a date, location, and the company it's for. Parents sign their dancers up from their portal. Review each signup with Accept, Waitlist, or Decline.
  • Performances - shows and events with a date, call time, and venue, plus the lineup of who's performing.
{% call tip("Attach the company when you create the audition") %} Accepting a dancer automatically adds them to the audition's company - but only if you set that company when creating the audition. Without it, accepting adds them to nothing and you'll have to add them by hand. {% endcall %}

Closing an audition stops new signups and is reversible. Deleting one removes its signups permanently, so close rather than delete unless you really mean it.

Performances created here are also what recital ticket sales attach to.

12. Staff & time clock

Staff accounts

Admin only: staff management, leads, and payroll reports. All staff can use their own time clock.

Staff is where you add teachers and admins and set who's who. Use Add Teacher and select Teacher or Admin, then enter the staff member's contact details, username, and initial password. Share credentials privately and ask them to change their password under Your Profile. Edit updates their details, role, or password; leaving the password field blank keeps the existing one. Give new instructors a teacher account unless they need to handle money. Deactivating a staff account signs them out immediately, including any session they already had open.

Time clock

Staff clock themselves in and out on the Time Clock page - one button, and it shows their recent shifts. Admins get a Payroll Hours section: pick a date range, press Run, and get hours per staff member with a total.

{% call warn("A missed clock-out won't show up in payroll") %} There's no way to edit or delete a punch in the app, and a shift that was never clocked out is left out of the payroll report entirely. If someone's hours look low, check whether they have a shift still sitting open. Text Thomas if a punch needs correcting. {% endcall %}

Hours are straight clock-out minus clock-in. No break deductions, no overtime rules, no pay rates - it's a record of hours worked, not a payroll processor.

Leads & Trials

Track prospective families from first contact through to enrollment. Add a lead with their contact info, what they're interested in, and how they found you, then move them along the stages: new, contacted, trial scheduled, converted, lost. Convert turns a lead into a real family and dancer.

Use the stage dropdown on a lead's row as the conversation progresses. Converting creates the family and dancer; it does not select a class or set up a parent login. Enroll the dancer and generate a portal invite afterward. Deleting a lead removes that lead record.

{% call tip("The list defaults to New only") %} When you open Leads & Trials you're seeing only brand-new leads, not everything. Change the filter to All if you're wondering where everyone went. {% endcall %}

13. Reports & donations

Admin only: studio reports, donations, and giving statements for other donors.

The four reports

  • Insights - active dancers, new this month, at risk, inactive, plus enrollment and attendance trends and your biggest classes.
  • Revenue - collected this month, this year, and all-time, billed versus collected by month, and a breakdown by category. Exports to CSV for your accountant.
  • Aging (A/R) - who owes what, split into 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ days, sorted by who owes most. This is your collections worklist.
  • Payments - the live ledger of everything charged and paid.
{% call tip("Revenue and Aging totals won't match, and that's correct") %} Revenue's "Outstanding" counts only active dancers. Aging also includes withdrawn dancers who still owe, marked "Withdrawn." If you're chasing money, trust Aging. {% endcall %}

On Aging, linked payments settle their checked charges first. Payments with nothing checked, and any part of a payment left over, settle the oldest unpaid charges in the same fund. Paying a newer item can leave an older one in the 90+ column. See Apply a payment to specific charges.

Review the Studio and Company totals and fund labels in each report. Revenue's collection totals exclude internal transfer entries, so moving credit does not count as new money collected. Aging keeps each fund's debt separate. Use its CSV export when following up on unpaid accounts.

Donations and giving statements

Donations records gifts to the LSODance Foundation. Enter the donor's name, email, amount, and method. Donations parents submit through their portal arrive as pending and need you to Confirm them before they count.

Check the actual gift arrived before confirming it. Use Delete to remove a mistaken or duplicate donation record; this changes the giving statement and does not refund money through a payment provider.

At year end, type a donor's email and the year into the bar at the top and press Giving statement to produce their printable 501(c)(3) letter with your EIN and the "no goods or services" language. Set the org name and EIN once under Payment Methods → Foundation Donations.

{% call warn("A giving statement needs the donor's email") %} Statements are matched by exact donor email. A donation entered without one (or with a typo) can be missing from the donor's statement. Get the email at the time you record the gift; ask for help correcting a record if needed. Only confirmed gifts appear. Company fees are not donation records. {% endcall %}

14. Your data & backups

Scheduled backups

The project has a nightly offsite backup job scheduled for 07:00 UTC (about 3am or 2am in Michigan depending on daylight saving time), with a 90-day retention policy. Successful backups depend on that job's configuration and completion. Ask Thomas to verify the latest successful backup before relying on it for a recovery.

Taking your own copy

Payment Methods → Data & Backup → Download backup gives you a complete copy of the studio's database as a file. Worth grabbing before anything big - a season switch, a bulk charge - and keeping somewhere safe. It's also your proof that this data is yours.

Admin only: full database downloads. They include sensitive family, staff, health, and billing records; keep them private. Restoring a backup requires technical help; uploading it into the portal is not supported.

Exports

  • Students → Export CSV - the roster
  • Revenue → Export CSV - every transaction, for the accountant
  • Aging → Export CSV - who owes what, with a total row

Privacy requests

Parents can download everything the studio holds on their family from their profile page, and can submit a deletion request. A deletion request doesn't erase anything by itself - it notifies admins so they can review the request and record retention before responding. The public privacy policy is at Privacy policy.
