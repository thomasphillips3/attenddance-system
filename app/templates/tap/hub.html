{% call card('Staff phone setup', 'list-ol') %}

iPhone (XS or newer)

  1. Sign in to this site in Safari and check "Remember me". Tag links open in Safari, and the Home Screen app keeps its own separate sign-in.
  2. Set Auto-Lock to 5 minutes or more during drop-off. A locked phone doesn't read tags.
  3. Close the Camera and Wallet. The iPhone won't read tags while either is open.
  4. Hold the top edge of the phone to the tag, then tap the banner that pops up. The result fills the screen.

Android

  1. Turn on NFC in Settings.
  2. In Chrome, open Tap-In, then Start a tap station, then tap Start. Keep the page open.
  3. Dancers hold their tag to the middle of the back of the phone. No banner to tap.

Setting up new tags needs an Android phone, or the free NXP TagWriter app on iPhone.
